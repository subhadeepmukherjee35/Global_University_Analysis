# Global University & Higher Education Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard for analyzing global higher-education data** across countries, universities, ranking systems, student demographics, and yearly trends.

The dashboard analyzes **1,247 universities, 26M+ total students, 4.78M international students, and 12M+ female students**.

The objective was to transform university-level data into meaningful insights that can support analysis of **global education patterns, university performance, international student mobility, enrollment trends, and ranking-system differences**.

## 🎯 Business Questions

The dashboard answers questions such as:

* Which countries have the highest number of universities?
* Which countries attract the most international students?
* Which countries have the highest female student enrollment?
* Which universities have the largest student populations?
* How do major university ranking systems differ?
* Which ranking system covers the largest number of universities?
* Is university score associated with international student enrollment?
* Is ranking score associated with female student representation?
* How does the student-staff ratio vary across universities and ranking systems?
* How have international and female student populations changed over time?
* What trends can be observed in university rankings?

## 🔍 Key Findings

### Country Analysis

The United States has the highest university count in the dataset, with **273 universities**, and also records the largest total student population.

### International Students

The U.S. has approximately **1.48M international students**, followed by the UK and Australia.

### Female Students

The United States has approximately **4.93M female students**, representing the largest female student population among the countries analyzed.

### Ranking Analysis

The ranking systems have different levels of university coverage:

| Ranking System                       | Universities |
| ------------------------------------ | -----------: |
| Center for World University Rankings |        1,024 |
| Times Higher Education               |          245 |
| Shanghai Ranking                     |           93 |

### Correlation Analysis

The dashboard identifies:

* University score vs. international students: **0.59 correlation**
* Ranking score vs. female student percentage: **0.62 correlation**
* Ranking criteria vs. student-staff ratio: **0.74 correlation**

These values indicate associations within the dataset and should not be interpreted as causal relationships.

### Time-Series Analysis

International student enrollment generally increased from 2005 to 2015, reaching approximately **4.76M**, followed by a decline in 2016.

Female student enrollment also increased substantially over the period before declining in 2016.
